Required controls

  • Legal-entity and beneficial-owner identification
  • Denied, restricted, sanctioned and debarred-party screening
  • End user, consignee, freight forwarder and destination review
  • ECCN / USML / license determination or documented NLR rationale
  • Commercial, industrial, marine, military and display-use separation
  • Serial, lot, life, trace, condition and release-document review
  • Payment, shipping and record-retention controls

Automatic hold triggers

  • Unknown end user or beneficial owner
  • Inconsistent route, destination, application or payment source
  • Military or controlled item without classification
  • Industrial or marine history represented as flight eligibility
  • Missing trace, altered identity, unreadable serials or conflicting records
  • Refusal to sign an End User Certificate
  • Pressure to bypass screening, paperwork or licensing

Human-gated agents

Search every direction.

These lanes define what the future outreach agents must research and draft. No agent may contact, quote, publish, represent eligibility or commit inventory without human review.

INBOUND

RFQ Match Agent

Parse incoming P/N, NSN, alternates, quantity, condition, due date, end use and destination; match stock and sourcing lanes; place elevated-risk requests on hold.

OUTBOUND

Buyer Match Agent

Identify operators, MROs, distributors, industrial users, shipyards, government contractors, collectors and repair organizations that can legitimately use the specific material.

ACQUISITION

Old Bearing Recovery Agent

Find surplus, as-removed and repairable bearings before they are scrapped; request identity, condition, photos, trace, lots and location.

REPAIR

Refurbishment Router

Route candidate bearings to approved inspection or repair partners, compare lead time and cost, and preserve chain of custody without publishing private partner terms.

GOVERNMENT

Contract Opportunity Agent

Monitor verified federal, defense and public procurement sources for exact P/N, NSN, engine and bearing requirements; enforce registration, representations and export gates.

SPECIAL PROJECTS

Legacy Engine Placement Agent

Find qualified museum, collector, engineering, film, land-speed, architectural and estate opportunities for legacy engines after provenance, demilitarization and transfer review.
