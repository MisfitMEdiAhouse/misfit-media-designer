Trust is a transaction control

Protect the part. Protect the money.

IALS separates commercial, industrial, marine, military, research, display and core material; verifies buyer and end-use information; preserves traceability; and places transactions on hold when identity, documentation, licensing, eligibility or destination concerns remain unresolved.

Compliance workflow

Default to HOLD.

Release happens only after the required case review. No website, agent, salesperson or marketplace listing can override the compliance decision.

Know the Buyer

Legal entity, beneficial owner, contact authority, address, payment source and transaction purpose.

Screen the Parties

Buyer, end user, consignee, freight forwarder, beneficial owners and destination against applicable restrictions.

Know the End Use

Aircraft, engine, industrial installation, marine platform, military program, research, display or other stated application.

Classify the Item

Document export classification, military or dual-use status, license determination and technical-data restrictions.

Verify the Records

Part identity, serial/lot, condition, trace, life records, chain of custody, release documents and ownership.

Officer Release

Human approval, documented conditions, payment verification, shipping controls and record retention.

End User Certificate

Written intent. Written accountability.

IALS may require a signed End User & Export Compliance Certification for international, military, dual-use, unusual-route, high-value or otherwise elevated-risk transactions.

Certificate captures

  • Purchaser and end user
  • Consignee and freight forwarder
  • Part numbers, quantities and serials
  • Destination and application
  • Detailed intended end use
  • Classification and license determination
  • No-diversion and no-prohibited-use representations

Certificate does not replace

  • Legal advice
  • Government authorization or export license
  • Airworthiness or interchangeability determination
  • Sanctions and denied-party screening
  • Traceability and release-document review
  • Human compliance-officer approval
IALS reserves the right to decline, suspend or cancel any inquiry or transaction when documentation, identity, payment, ownership, end use, destination, licensing or eligibility cannot be satisfactorily verified.
