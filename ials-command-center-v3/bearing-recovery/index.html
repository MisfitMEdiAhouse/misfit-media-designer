Value recovery workflow

Do not scrap the identity.

The part number, serial or lot, packaging, storage history, photographs, trace and damage condition may determine whether a bearing remains marketable, repairable, useful as a core or suitable only for non-service applications.

1. Identify

Primary P/N, alternate P/N, NSN, manufacturer, serial/lot, engine or accessory position and known application.

2. Preserve

Keep packaging, tags, paperwork, receipts, chain-of-custody records and photographs. Do not clean, polish or alter markings without instructions.

3. Evaluate

Screen for documentation, storage, corrosion, damage, dimensional concerns, repair options, buyer demand and lawful end use.

4. Route

Send qualified candidates to a private inspection or repair partner under controlled commercial terms and chain of custody.

5. Reclassify

Commercial aviation eligible, commercial-origin industrial use, industrial/marine only, military/controlled, or unverified/hold.

6. Market

Match the verified outcome to an operator, MRO, repair shop, distributor, industrial user, exchange program or core buyer.
